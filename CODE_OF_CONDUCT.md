# Code of conduct

## Our standard

FactorFoundry is a research project. Disagreement is expected; rigor is required.
We welcome participants who critique ideas, assumptions, code, and evidence
without attacking people.

Examples of positive participation:

- reproduce a result and report the exact environment and run metadata;
- challenge a factor's economic interpretation with a concrete falsification test;
- point out a leakage path, cost assumption, or statistical error;
- help a contributor turn an informal claim into a testable protocol.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
deliberate misrepresentation of results, publishing private data or credentials,
and presenting research output as guaranteed investment advice.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and any project-affiliated communication.

## Reporting

Do not publish credentials, private market data, or sensitive personal
information in an issue. Report conduct or security concerns privately through
the repository's security reporting channel or by contacting the maintainer via
GitHub.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
